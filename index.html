The Method

A Structured Path to Clarity

Path Integration Coaching uses a proven four-phase framework to help you move from feeling stuck to taking aligned, confident action. Each phase builds on the last — so your plan fits who you truly are.

Purpose Discovery

Uncover what truly drives you beneath the noise. We clarify your values, interests, and non-negotiables to build a foundation for every decision that follows.

Strengths & Flow

Identify your natural talents and the conditions where you perform at your best. We use evidence-based assessments to map your unique strengths profile.

Change Barriers

Name the patterns, fears, and stories that keep you stuck. We work through what's actually blocking your progress — not just what's on the surface.

Action & Accountability

Build a realistic, step-by-step plan with built-in checkpoints. You leave each session with clear next steps — and the follow-through to match.

Tools for Insight

Understanding You, Deeply

These professionally validated assessments reduce guesswork and help build a plan based on who you actually are — not who you think you should be.

Strong Interest Inventory

Maps your interests against career themes and work environments to identify paths that genuinely engage you — backed by decades of research.

VIA Character Strengths

Reveals your core character strengths — the qualities that come most naturally to you and drive satisfaction when you use them regularly.

Work Values Inventory

Clarifies what matters most to you in your work life — autonomy, security, impact, creativity — so your next move aligns with your real priorities.

Differential Aptitude Tests

Measures your natural cognitive strengths across areas like reasoning, spatial awareness, and language to match opportunities to your abilities.

Who This Is For

You might be ready if...

  • You feel stuck or overwhelmed, but you can't quite name why
  • You're in a major life transition — career change, empty nest, divorce, post-college — and you need a clear path forward
  • You want clarity about what you actually want, not just what looks good on paper
  • You've tried figuring it out on your own, but you keep going in circles
  • You're ready for structured support — not vague advice, but a real plan
